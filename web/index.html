Command Handbook

Simple, Intuitive & Fast Commands

Use the default prefix ! or slash / commands inside Discord.

Command Aliases Description Action
/setdj <role> or !setdj !djrole Configures the server DJ role required for web playback & control.
/join or !join !j, !connect Summons the bot to your current voice channel immediately.
/play <query> or !play !p, !sing Plays a song from YouTube query or direct audio link.
/pause or !pause — Pauses current music stream immediately.
/resume or !resume !unpause Resumes paused playback.
/skip or !skip !s, !next Skips the currently playing song to the next in queue.
/queue or !queue !q Shows the list of upcoming songs in the server queue.
/nowplaying or !np !current Displays rich embed with artwork, author, and track duration.
/volume <0-100> !vol Adjusts output volume without distortion.
/loop or !loop !repeat Toggles single song looping on or off.
/stop or !stop !clear Stops music playback and clears upcoming queue.
/leave or !leave !dc, !disconnect Disconnects the bot from voice channel.
